Academics & Accountability

Domain 3 — TELPAS Impact & Campus Improvement Plans

The Executive Committee reviewed the district-created Domain 3: Closing the Gaps dashboard and analyzed the preliminary findings. Certain campuses scored 0/4 on the TELPAS (English Language Proficiency) component, which requires targeted interventions.

📊 Targeted Support Sessions:
• Leadership Support Series: Wednesday, Aug 26 (9:00 AM – 10:00 AM) to review dashboards and intervention strategies.
• ESF Self-Assessment: Identified campuses will draft customized improvement plans supported by the Regional Director of Academics by Thursday, September 10, 2026.

Leadership & Appraisals

T-PESS & T-TESS Evaluation Framework Rollout

As part of our commitment to instructional excellence, we are rolling out T-PESS (Texas Principal Evaluation and Support System) appraiser certifications. Certification is mandatory prior to conducting formal observations that impact Teacher Incentive Allotment (TIA) designations.

Operations & Policies

Staffing, Titles & Communication Standards

👥 Teacher Aide FTE Model: Executive leadership is developing a guide to utilize dedicated Teacher Aide FTEs to mitigate daily substitute shortages. Talent Acquisition will distribute details soon.
🏷️ Leadership Title Alignment: Transition from "Dean" titles to official Assistant Principal titles (e.g., Assistant Principal of Academics, Assistant Principal of Discipline & Safety, Assistant Principal of CCMR).
✉️ Standardized Email Signatures: Staff should update signatures to reflect core district values in place of personal character quotes. Central Office staff serving shared service agreements must display approved partner logos.
